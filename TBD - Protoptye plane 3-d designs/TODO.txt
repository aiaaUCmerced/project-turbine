moved wing leading edge to 2in behind nose
	rationale: reduces torque from monoprop + gives back space from increased wing area + pushes cg forward for more layout flexibilty

revised brackets to have top and bottom support separated by 1/4in. increased internal width from 4in to 4.25 because of shrinkage
	rationale: form-fitting brackets were warping the fuselage and gap can be filled with glue anyway. manufacurability was difficult from warping

wing depth increased, wing chord to 5.25 + aileron to 1.5 -> total width to 13.25in
	rationale: increased wing area due to lack of lift and control authority

landing gear configuration changed to tail dragging, with recessed wheel in the back (sticking out of the top) and front landing gear mounted directly to the nose (somehow)
	rationale: front landing gear tbd. configuration changed due to infeasibility of tricycle landing gear from testing. 

added 4 bulkheads: two flanking wing front and back, two flanking back access panel.
	rationale: fuselage warping from collisions, so added to provide additional structural support. also provides channels for routing wiring.
